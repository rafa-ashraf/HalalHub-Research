Product Data, Matching & Image Strategy

A concise visual review of the HalalHub research: the problem, architecture, industry lessons, matching strategy, image governance, AI/data sources, risks, costs and roadmap.

60-second summary

Central catalog

Build a canonical product layer shared across merchants.

Identity first

Use GTIN/UPC as the strongest deterministic identifier, then metadata and AI-assisted matching.

Images as assets

Prioritize trusted catalog/manufacturer imagery and govern provenance and quality.

Selective AI

Use AI for ambiguous matching, enhancement and background cleanup rather than as the source of truth.

Image governance

1 · Trusted catalog

Preferred when available.

2 · Manufacturer / brand

High-confidence source.

3 · Licensed provider

Use where rights and quality are clear.

4 · Merchant

Useful fallback with validation.

5 · AI enhancement

Improve existing assets; don't replace truth with invented product imagery.

6 · Human review

Escalation for uncertain cases.

AI, APIs & cost strategy

Use Open Food Facts, GS1, Google Shopping and commercial/manufacturer sources where appropriate. Keep image processing asynchronous, use object storage/CDN, and stage external-data and AI costs rather than applying expensive processing to every item.

Risks → controls

Incorrect matching

Confidence thresholds + human review.

AI-generated errors

Use AI selectively and preserve source provenance.

Copyright / licensing

Track source and usage rights.

Scalability

Async pipelines, caching and external image storage.
